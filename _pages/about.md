    <h2>Hi, I'm Suhas!</h2>
    
    <p>I'm an Applied Scientist at Amazon, based in the Pacific Northwest. Previously, I completed my Ph.D. in Informatics and M.S. in Electrical Engineering at Penn State, advised by <a href="https://saeedabdullah.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Dr. Saeed Abdullah</a>, where I built AI tools for mental health therapy, including synthetic therapy conversations and empathetic small language models for PTSD treatment.</p>
    
    <p>My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, signal processing, and human wellbeing, from using speech signals to detect neurological conditions and building privacy-preserving depression assessment tools to creating sensor networks to prevent train derailments in India.</p>

<h2 id="research">Research</h2>

I work at the intersection of **Machine Learning**, **Human-Computer Interaction**, and **Healthcare**, developing AI systems that are both clinically meaningful and privacy-preserving.

- **Synthetic Therapy Data**: Created *Thousand Voices of Trauma*, a dataset of 3,000+ synthetic PTSD therapy conversations with clinical validation
- **Empathetic AI**: Evaluated and fine-tuned small language models for trauma-informed, empathetic dialogue support
- **Automated Clinical Assessment**: Built audio-language models for automatic temporal localization of therapy fidelity elements
- **Privacy-Preserving Methods**: Extended federated learning and differential privacy approaches for sensitive healthcare applications
- **Healthcare Applications**: Cuff-less blood pressure monitoring and speech-based neurological disorder detection

<details class="news-more">
  <summary>Academic Journey &amp; Co-authorship Distances</summary>
  <div style="margin-top: 0.75rem;">
    <p>I graduated from PES University in Bangalore, India, with a B.Tech in Electronics &amp; Communication Engineering, specializing in Signal Processing, and a minor in Computer Science. My thesis focused on using wireless sensor networks to prevent train derailments in India, and I was fortunate to be advised by Gp Capt. (Retd.) Suresh Padmanabhan.</p>

    <p>Our work caught the attention of Shri. Piyush Goyal, the then Hon'ble Minister for Railways in India, who mentioned our approach (<a href="https://www.financialexpress.com/infrastructure/railways/building-a-smarter-indian-railways-piyush-goyal-highlights-7-projects-that-will-transform-rail-network/1297063/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Building smarter (and safer) Indian Railways</a>) in his plans for improving railway safety throughout the country. We were also able to present our work at the <a href="https://doi.org/10.1109/CETIC4.2018.8531029" target="_blank" rel="noopener">IEEE IC4 2018</a> conference in Thiruvananthapuram, India.</p>

    <p>I was lucky to have the opportunity to work at the Indian Institute of Science in Bangalore on a project sponsored by the Department of Science and Technology (DST, Govt. of India). The project aimed to use speech signal processing to identify neurological conditions such as Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis and Parkinson's disease. As part of my assistantship, I worked with patients at the National Institute of Mental Health and Neurosciences (NIMHANS), collecting and analyzing their speech samples. My advisor was <a href="https://ee.iisc.ac.in/prasanta-kumar-ghosh/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Dr. Prasanta Kumar Ghosh</a>, and we published our findings at <a href="https://www.isca-speech.org/archive/Interspeech_2019/abstracts/1285.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">INTERSPEECH 2019</a>, <a href="https://doi.org/10.1109/ICASSP40776.2020.9053682" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ICASSP 2020</a>, and <a href="https://doi.org/10.1109/SPCOM50965.2020.9179503" target="_blank" rel="noopener">SPCOM 2020</a> (<a href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1v94UzLJoqQb-5WJRyxcWXZegIgHAcohf/view?usp=sharing" target="_blank" rel="noopener">presentation video</a>).</p>

    <p>At Penn State, I collaborated with researchers across the School of EECS and the College of IST. For my <a href="https://etda.libraries.psu.edu/files/final_submissions/23854" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Master's thesis</a>, I worked with Dr. Saeed Abdullah (with Dr. David Miller and Dr. Jing Yang on my thesis committee) to explore how federated learning can preserve privacy while assessing depression (<a href="https://doi.org/10.1109/ICASSP43922.2022.9746827" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ICASSP 2022</a>).</p>
